## Context

For the same health insurer as the [member portal](/en/case-studies/portal-tramites/),
I used **Next.js** to build the platform where members find and redeem benefits at
partner businesses. They search by province and city or by their current
location, and results appear in a **list** and on a map powered by **Google Maps**. The
design is **mobile-first**.

## The challenge: a map you can use with your thumb

A map with a few pins is easy. The problems show up with real data: businesses
sharing an address, many results close together, and two views (list and map)
that have to show the same thing.

## What I solved

- **Shared locations.** When several businesses are in the same building or
  mall, their pins overlap and can't be tapped separately. I grouped them by
  coordinates: the map shows a single pin and, instead of a single card,
  opens a **carousel** with every business at that place.
- **Synced list and map.** Both views share the selected business. From any
  business in the list, "Show on map" takes you straight to its pin on the map
  with its card open.
- **Density.** With many results, pins merge into **clusters showing the count**,
  and members can move the map and **search this area**.

Grouping by coordinates, in its simplest form:

```js
const groups = businesses.reduce((acc, b) => {
  const key = `${b.lat},${b.lng}`
  ;(acc[key] ||= []).push(b)
  return acc
}, {})
```

## The member's journey

The journey ends with redemption: from the benefit details, members open a QR code
